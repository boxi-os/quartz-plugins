How it fits together

  • Three steps, the middle one with a condition: whoever picks “Sadly not” jumps from step 1 straight to “Almost done”, and the count says “Step 2 of 2: Almost done” (4.2).
  • A chain in the middle step: “How many children?” appears only with the “With children” box ticked, and is required only then (4.1).
  • The consent in the last step, which has no condition. In a step with a condition the build would refuse it.
  • "yes" and "no" in quotes: they are not needed, Quartz reads the config as YAML 1.2, where a bare yes stays a word. Older YAML readers (YAML 1.1) make a boolean of it, though; in quotes the value is the same everywhere.
  • Name and e-mail side by side, stacked on a phone (2.3).

The message

For an acceptance, for instance:

Name: Ada Lovelace
E-mail: ada@example.org
Are you coming?: Yes
People in all: 3
We bring: Salad, Cake
[x] With children
How many children?: 1
[x] I agree that my details are stored to organise the event.

For a refusal every line of the middle step is missing.
