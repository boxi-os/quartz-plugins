This website holds the handbooks of four Quartz plugins, and it is built with all four:

- [Layout Box]({{root}}/en/layout-box/): this box here
- [Multilanguage]({{root}}/en/multilanguage/): the switcher at the top right
- [Navigations]({{root}}/en/navigations/): the menu on the left and “Previous” and “Next” below the text
- [Forms]({{root}}/en/forms/): the forms that send nothing but show what would have arrived
